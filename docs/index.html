Why Tier 3 fails

Gemini and Grok applied the prescribed from-scratch multi-scale network to the letter. ChatGPT and Claude swapped it for a pretrained ResNet18. A 2×2 factorial design separates the two possible causes.

Ablation: network × scheduler
Conclusion

The pretrained network reaches 0.945–0.947 with either scheduler. The from-scratch network stays at 0.53–0.57, close to chance. Training from scratch on 478 images decides the outcome, not the scheduler.

5 runs × 20 epochs per cell, with the same split, augmentation, optimizer and early stopping. The vertical line marks 0.5.
